Guitar and bass samples
=======================
The recordings in this folder (gtr_*.ogg: acoustic guitar, bass_*.ogg: electric bass)
come from "tonejs-instruments" by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky
(https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The acoustic guitar was originally recorded by the University of Iowa Electronic
Music Studios, the electric bass by Karoryfer Samples.

Changes made for Key Compass: trimmed (silence before the note, length capped at
3.6 s for guitar and 4.5 s for bass, with a fade-out), converted to mono and
re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. samples.txt lists each note and its measured tuning,
which the plug-in corrects when it plays them.
