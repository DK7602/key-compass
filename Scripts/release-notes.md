**Key Compass @VERSION@** - the phone / tablet app and the PC plug-in and app.

**Phone or tablet** (Android 8 or newer): open this page on the phone, tap **KeyCompass.apk**, then *Open*. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser - allow it, then tap *Install*. A new version installs over the old one and keeps your songs.

**PC** (Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit): download **KeyCompass-Windows.zip**, extract it, close Cubase, and double-click **Install Key Compass.bat** (READ ME FIRST.txt explains). It installs the VST3 plug-in and the standalone app.

To keep your songs the same on both: *My Songs > CLOUD SYNC* (see the manual).
